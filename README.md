# Awesome-AI-Interview-Platform

## Top AI Interview Platform Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on AI-Powered Hiring Interviews, Voice Screening, Structured Assessment, Proctoring & Candidate Evaluation*  
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Interview Platforms**. These systems conduct or assist video/voice interviews, score responses, support coding assessments, and help recruiters screen candidates at scale with structured, consistent evaluation.

**Examples** include HireVue, Apriora, Paradox Interviewer, Micro1 Interview AI, Metaview AI, GoodTime AI Interview, Barraiser, Final Round AI, CodeSignal AI Interview, and Screenloop AI (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Enterprise hiring interview products are mostly commercial. Open projects such as **FoloUp** and community AI interview platforms provide self-hosted voice interviewers, scoring, and practice tools. This section lists every significant relevant project found.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[HireVue](https://www.hirevue.com/)**  
  Established AI video interview and assessment platform used by large employers for structured screening and evaluation.

- **[Paradox Interviewer, Apriora, Micro1](https://www.paradox.ai/)**  
  Conversational and AI interview products focused on high-volume screening, scheduling, and candidate experience.

- **[Metaview AI, GoodTime, Screenloop](https://www.metaview.ai/)**  
  Platforms that record, summarize, and analyze live interviews—AI notes, scorecards, and insights for recruiting teams.

- **[Barraiser, Final Round AI, CodeSignal AI Interview](https://www.codesignal.com/)**  
  Tools spanning interview coaching, practice, technical assessment, and AI-assisted evaluation for hiring and candidates.

- **[Other commercial AI interview platforms](https://www.hirevue.com/)**  
  Additional solutions for async video interviews, skills testing, and recruiter copilots.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[FoloUp](https://github.com/foloup/foloup)**  
  Open-source AI-powered voice interviewer for hiring—generate questions from job descriptions, share interview links, conduct conversational AI interviews, and score responses with a dashboard (MIT).

- **[AI interview platform prototypes](https://github.com/vijaygupta18/ai-interview-platform)**  
  Full-stack open projects featuring voice/video interviews, proctoring signals, multi-dimensional scoring, and candidate management for learning and customization.

- **[MockMate & practice interview simulators](https://github.com/sythe95/mockmate)**  
  Open AI interview simulators with voice conversation, resume-grounded questions, and post-session analytics—useful for candidate practice and EdTech.

- **[Assess-AI / LiveKit interview agents](https://github.com/Ashmit-Kumar/Interviewer-agent)**  
  Open end-to-end stacks combining live voice agents (STT/LLM/TTS), coding assessment, and automated feedback.

- **[Speech-to-text & diarization open stacks](https://github.com/openai/whisper)**  
  Whisper and related open STT tools used as the transcription backbone for custom interview analysis.

- **[Coding assessment open platforms](https://github.com/search?q=coding+interview+OR+online+judge+open+source)**  
  Open coding challenge and judge systems that can pair with AI interview flows for technical roles.

- **[Scorecard & rubric open templates](https://github.com/search?q=interview+scorecard+OR+hiring+rubric)**  
  Structured evaluation templates and simple apps for consistent human or AI-assisted scoring.

- **[Proctoring research components](https://github.com/search?q=online+proctoring+OR+face+detection+interview)**  
  Open face/gaze/window-focus detection experiments sometimes used in integrity modules (use carefully and lawfully).

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **Hiring-oriented**: FoloUp as a ready open voice interviewer with sharing and scoring.
- **Practice / EdTech**: MockMate-style simulators for candidates and training.
- **Custom stacks**: Whisper + LLM interviewer + TTS + scorecard for a DIY AI interview loop.
- **Technical roles**: Open coding judges + AI follow-up questions.
- Commercial platforms still lead in enterprise ATS integration, compliance, and validated assessments.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  
**FoloUp** and similar open interview apps are the closest product-like open options.  
Build custom flows with **Whisper** (STT) + LLM + TTS + structured scorecards.  
Commercial platforms (HireVue, Paradox, Metaview, CodeSignal, etc.) provide scale, legal review, and ATS depth.  
Startups and internal tools may self-host open interviewers; large employers typically use commercial AI interview products for consistency and compliance. Fully open stacks work for practice and low-stakes screening with careful process design.

## Disclaimer

- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
- AI interviews raise fairness, bias, privacy, and employment-law issues. Automated scores should not be the sole basis for hiring decisions without human review and validated processes. Comply with EEOC, GDPR, local consent, and recording laws.
- Open-source tools offer control but require you to handle security, bias testing, and candidate rights. Commercial platforms shift some compliance and support burden to the vendor. Involve legal and HR before production use.
